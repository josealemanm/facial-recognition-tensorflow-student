# Third-party notices

This repository contains no third-party model weights, datasets, or copied
source files.

The project depends on TensorFlow, Keras, NumPy, Pillow, scikit-learn, joblib,
and optionally OpenCV. Those packages retain their own licenses. TensorFlow's
optional MobileNetV2 weights are downloaded through Keras at runtime and are
not redistributed here.

The learning sequence was inspired by Cole Murray's 2017 HackerNoon tutorial
and companion repository. Face embeddings were popularized by the FaceNet
paper by Florian Schroff, Dmitry Kalenichenko, and James Philbin. Full links
and provenance are in ATTRIBUTION.md.
